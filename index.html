Analyst Interpretation

Supply margin is calculated as available generation minus total demand. Lower supply margin indicates tighter operating conditions and greater sensitivity to outages, renewable reductions, interconnector changes, or ramp limits.

Event Classes

Event Class Analyst Meaning
Supply Tightness EventSupply margin is tight relative to demand, increasing scarcity pricing risk.
Generator Ramping EventAggregate generator movement is elevated, indicating material supply-side response.
High Volatility EventRegional price volatility is elevated, indicating unstable market conditions.
Oversupply EventNegative prices and healthy supply margin suggest surplus generation pressure.
Renewable Dominance EventNegative price with elevated aggregate output, used as a renewable dominance proxy.
Mixed Driver EventMultiple market signals are active, so a single-driver explanation may be misleading.

Methodology

The project is built as a transparent analyst workflow from raw dispatch data to decision intelligence.

Workflow

StepPurpose
01 Data ExtractionExtract and clean price, region summary, and SCADA data from PostgreSQL.
02 Feature EngineeringCreate supply margin, volatility, generator ramping, and utilisation proxy features.
03-06 AnalysisAnalyse generation mix, ramping, supply adequacy, and generator event response.
07 ClassificationClassify intervals into transparent event driver categories.
08 Decision IntelligenceGenerate recommendations, risks, and confidence levels.
09 VisualisationCreate interactive Plotly dashboard charts.

Limitations And Extensions

This version analyses physical generator behaviour from SCADA and regional market data. It does not yet include bid data, fuel type mapping, storage classification, or network constraint context.
